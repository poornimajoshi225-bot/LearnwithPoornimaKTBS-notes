Chapter 1

Treasure Island

By Robert Louis Stevenson

About the Author

Robert Louis Stevenson (1850–1894) was a famous Scottish novelist, poet, and travel writer. He is best known for his adventure novels, especially Treasure Island, which is one of the world's greatest adventure stories. His works are loved for their exciting characters, suspense, and memorable storytelling.

Introduction

Treasure Island is an exciting adventure novel about pirates, hidden treasure, courage, and friendship. The story follows young Jim Hawkins as he discovers a mysterious treasure map and joins a dangerous voyage to find Captain Flint's hidden treasure. Along the way, he faces many challenges and encounters the clever pirate Long John Silver.

Theme

  • Adventure
  • Courage
  • Greed
  • Friendship
  • Loyalty

Moral

Courage, honesty, and wisdom help people overcome even the greatest dangers. Greed often leads to destruction.

II. COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS

A. Answer briefly the following questions

1. What was Admiral Benbow?

Admiral Benbow was an inn kept by Jim Hawkins' father and mother, located as the only building in a lonely cove in the west of England.

2. Who was the guest who took up a room in Admiral Benbow?

A wizened old seaman with a saber-scar across his face, who called himself Billy, took a room at the inn.

3. What was his cherished possession?

His sole and cherished possession was an old sea-chest, which he guarded carefully.

4. Why did he give Jim four pence?

He gave Jim four pence to keep a watch for a seafaring man with one leg.

5. Why was Billy looking out to sea every evening?

Billy feared that his old pirate companions, especially the one-legged sailor, would come searching for him.

6. Describe the encounter between Billy and the mysterious seafaring man.

A strange sailor with two fingers missing argued with Billy. They fought fiercely, the stranger was wounded and escaped, while Billy suffered a stroke.

7. What is the black spot?

The black spot is a pirate warning or summons used to accuse or condemn a pirate.

8. What did Jim and his mother take from Billy's sea-chest before they fled the inn?

Jim's mother took only the money due for the rent, while Jim took a bundle of papers wrapped in oilcloth.

9. What important document did the bundle of papers contain?

It contained Captain Flint's treasure map showing the location of the buried treasure.

10. Who did Jim give it to?

Jim gave the papers to Squire Trelawney and Dr. Livesey.

11. How did Blind Pew die?

Blind Pew was trampled to death by the horses of the revenue officers.

12. What arrangement did Squire Trelawney and Dr. Livesey make for a treasure hunt?

They bought the schooner Hispaniola, hired a crew, and planned a voyage to Treasure Island.

13. There are two Captain Flints in the story. Who are they?

One is the famous pirate Captain Flint, and the other is Long John Silver's parrot named Captain Flint.

14. Who was nicknamed Barbecue? Why?

Long John Silver was nicknamed "Barbecue" because he served as the ship's cook.

15. Why was Capt. Smollett unhappy?

He disliked the crew chosen for the voyage and was unhappy that everyone knew about the treasure expedition.

16. What was John Silver's treacherous plan?

He planned to lead a mutiny after the treasure was found and seize it for himself.

17. How did Jim come to know about it?

Jim overheard Silver's conversation while hiding inside an apple barrel.

18. Why did Capt. Smollett say that the odds were stacked against their survival?

Because only a few loyal men remained compared to the large number of mutineers.

19. How does Jim manage to escape to the island?

He secretly boarded one of the boats going ashore and slipped away into the forest.

20. Who is Ben Gunn?

Ben Gunn is a former pirate who had been marooned on Treasure Island for three years.

21. Why and how was Ben left marooned on the island?

His companions abandoned him after they failed to find Captain Flint's treasure.

22. Why did Capt. Smollett and others consider the stockade an ideal place?

It was well protected with a strong fence, loopholes for muskets, and a secure log house.

23. What was Silver's demand for a truce? What was Capt. Smollett's response?

Silver demanded the treasure map in exchange for peace, but Capt. Smollett firmly refused.

24. What was the result of the attack on the stockade?

The pirates were defeated with heavy losses, though two loyal men died and Capt. Smollett was wounded.

25. What surprise awaited Jim when he returned to the stockade?

He found that Long John Silver and the pirates had taken control of it.

26. Give any two examples of Jim's acts of bravery.

Jim captured the Hispaniola and boldly faced Long John Silver and the pirates.

27. On what condition was Silver willing to surrender to Capt. Smollett?

He wanted assurance of fair and lenient treatment.

28. Why did the pirates serve the black spot on Silver?

They blamed him for their failures and wanted to remove him as leader.

29. Why had the doctor given the pirates the stockade and the treasure map?

Because Ben Gunn had already hidden the treasure elsewhere, making the map useless.

30. Why did the pirates accuse Silver and Jim of treachery?

They discovered that the treasure pit was empty and believed they had been deceived.

31. Why did the captain want to sail to the Spanish Americas?

He wanted to recruit more sailors because many crew members had been lost.

32. What did Silver do in the end?

Silver escaped with a small bag of gold coins and disappeared.

B. Discuss in groups of four each the following questions

1. Which character do you like the most? Why?

Sample Answer: Jim Hawkins is my favourite character because he is brave, intelligent, and always ready to face danger. His courage and presence of mind help everyone succeed in the treasure hunt.

2. Which incident in the story do you like the most? Why?

Sample Answer: I like the incident where Jim captures the Hispaniola. It is exciting because Jim risks his life, defeats the pirates, and safely brings the ship back to his friends.

14. Why does Indra offer a boon to Karna? What is the boon?

Answer: Indra was deeply moved by Karna's unmatched generosity after receiving his divine armour and earrings. As a reward, he granted Karna the powerful weapon Sakti, which could kill even the mightiest enemy.

15. Indra granted the boon with a condition. What was the condition?

Answer: Indra said that the Sakti weapon could be used only once. After killing one enemy, it would immediately return to Indra and could never be used again.

16. How did Parasurama realise that Karna was not a Brahmana?

Answer: While Parasurama was sleeping on Karna's lap, a worm bored into Karna's thigh. Karna silently endured the unbearable pain without moving. Seeing such extraordinary endurance, Parasurama realized that only a Kshatriya could bear such suffering and concluded that Karna had deceived him.

17. What was Parasurama's curse?

Answer: Parasurama cursed Karna that the knowledge of the Brahmastra would fail him at the most critical moment in battle when he needed it the most.

18. What does Bhishma ask Karna to do?

Answer: Bhishma advised Karna to make peace with the Pandavas because they were his own brothers. However, he finally blessed Karna to remain loyal to Duryodhana and fight bravely.

19. What does Karna say about his loyalty to Duryodhana?

Answer: Karna said that Duryodhana had trusted and honoured him when everyone else insulted him. Therefore, he would never betray his friend and would remain loyal to him even at the cost of his own life.

20. How does Lord Krishna save Arjuna from Karna's arrow?

Answer: Lord Krishna suddenly pressed Arjuna's chariot deep into the ground. As a result, Karna's deadly arrow missed Arjuna's head and only knocked off his crown.

21. What mishap happened to Karna's chariot?

Answer: During the fierce battle, one wheel of Karna's chariot got stuck in the muddy battlefield, making it impossible for him to continue fighting properly.

22. Mention any three unfair acts of Karna and the Kauravas recalled by Lord Krishna.

Answer:

  • They insulted and humiliated Draupadi in the royal court.
  • They cheated Yudhishthira in the game of dice.
  • They surrounded and unfairly killed Abhimanyu in battle.

23(a). What was Karna's response to Lord Krishna's denouncement?

Answer: Karna bowed his head in shame and remained completely silent.

23(b). What does this tell you about Karna?

Answer: It shows that Karna was honest and had a clear conscience. He accepted the truth instead of trying to justify his mistakes.

24. Why was Arjuna's mind wavering when Lord Krishna asked him to slay Karna?

Answer: Arjuna hesitated because Karna was unarmed and was trying to lift the wheel of his chariot. According to the rules of war, attacking such a warrior was considered unfair.

25. Was killing Karna when he was not ready for the fight wrong?

Answer: According to the traditional rules of warfare, it was wrong to attack an unarmed warrior. Therefore, Arjuna initially hesitated.

26. How does the poet justify this wrong?

Answer: The poet explains that Lord Krishna reminded Arjuna of all the unjust and cruel actions committed by Karna and the Kauravas. Krishna instructed Arjuna to kill Karna to uphold justice and destroy evil.

III. DISCUSSION QUESTIONS

Discuss in pairs and answer the following questions.

1. Karna is one of the most tragic and compelling characters in the Mahabharata. List any five great qualities of Karna which you admire most. Justify your choice.

Answer:

  1. Generosity: Karna was famous for his charity. He never refused anyone who sought help, even when it meant sacrificing his own divine armour and earrings.
  2. Loyalty: He remained faithful to Duryodhana throughout his life because Duryodhana supported him when others insulted him.
  3. Courage: Karna fought fearlessly against great warriors despite knowing the dangers that awaited him.
  4. Determination: He worked hard to become a great warrior and never allowed difficulties or discrimination to stop him.
  5. Humility: When Lord Krishna reminded him of his mistakes, Karna remained silent and accepted the truth with dignity.

2. Epics highlight eternal values. List any five values from this lesson that are relevant to our lives today.

Answer:

  • Gratitude – Always be thankful to those who help us.
  • Loyalty – Stand by true friends during difficult times.
  • Generosity – Help others without expecting anything in return.
  • Courage – Face difficulties with confidence and determination.
  • Justice and Fairness – Always follow truth and uphold righteousness in life.

The life of Karna teaches us that qualities such as honesty, sacrifice, friendship, kindness and courage are timeless values that inspire people even today.
